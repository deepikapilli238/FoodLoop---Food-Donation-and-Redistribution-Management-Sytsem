HOW IT WORKS

Food Sharing Made Simple

Three simple steps to connect surplus food with people who need it.

01
🍱

List Surplus Food

Restaurants, hostels and college canteens list their available surplus food with quantity, location and expiry time.

02
🔎

Find and Request

NGOs and food receivers explore available donations and request the food they need.

03
💚

Collect and Share

Donors approve requests and coordinate pickup. The completed donation can then be tracked.

ABOUT FOODLOOP

Turning Surplus Food Into Shared Hope

FoodLoop is a smart food surplus redistribution platform designed to reduce avoidable food waste and help connect food donors with NGOs and food receivers.

Our platform makes it easier to list food, request donations and coordinate pickups through one organized application.

  • ✓ Simple food donation management
  • ✓ Organized food request tracking
  • ✓ Clear donation and pickup statuses
  • ✓ Better coordination between donors and NGOs
